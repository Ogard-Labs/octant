# Code of Conduct

Octant is a small open-source project. We want it to be a place where people
can report bugs, propose ideas, review code, and disagree about design without
being made to feel unwelcome.

## Expectations

Everyone participating in this repository — issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any project space — is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be direct and constructive. Say what is wrong and, where you can, how to
  fix it.
- Welcome newcomers and questions. Nobody was born knowing this codebase.
- Respect maintainers' time and decisions. Not every proposal will be
  accepted; a "no" is not personal.
- Keep private information private. Do not share others' personal details,
  credentials, or private communications.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language, trolling,
sustained disruption, and doxxing are not acceptable here in any form.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that
violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently block participants for
behavior they judge harmful. Decisions are made by the maintainers and are
final.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub: open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/Ogard-Labs/octant/security/advisories/new)
on this repository (it reaches only the maintainers), or contact a maintainer
directly via their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
